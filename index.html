Creamy Garlic Pasta

Silky, garlicky pasta finished with parmesan, black pepper and fresh herbs. A simple recipe that feels special without taking all evening.

⏱️ PREP 10 min
🍳 COOK 20 min
👨‍🍳 SERVES 2 People

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti 200 g
  • Butter 2 tbsp
  • Garlic 4 cloves
  • Cooking cream 150 ml
  • Parmesan 50 g
  • Fresh parsley 2 tbsp
  • Black pepper ½ tsp
  • Salt To taste

Preparation

  1. Cook the pasta

    Boil salted water and cook the pasta until al dente. Reserve some pasta water before draining.

  2. Prepare the garlic

    Melt butter in a pan over medium heat. Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant.

  3. Make the sauce

    Add cream and black pepper. Simmer gently and stir in grated parmesan.

  4. Combine everything

    Add pasta to the sauce and toss well. Add pasta water if needed and finish with fresh parsley.

✦

Chef's Tip: Save a little pasta water before draining. It helps the sauce become glossy and creamy.
