Creamy Garlic Pasta

Silky, garlicky pasta finished with parmesan, black pepper and fresh herbs. A simple recipe that feels special without taking all evening.

⏱ PREP 10 min
🍳 COOK 20 min
👨‍🍳 SERVES 2 People

Ingredients

Simple ingredients for a creamy, comforting pasta.

  • Spaghetti 200 g
  • Butter 2 tbsp
  • Garlic 4 cloves
  • Cooking cream 150 ml
  • Parmesan 50 g
  • Fresh parsley 2 tbsp
  • Black pepper ½ tsp
  • Salt To taste

Preparation

Follow these steps in order for the best result.

  1. Cook the pasta

    Bring salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti until al dente. Reserve some pasta water before draining.

  2. Prepare the garlic

    Melt butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant.

  3. Make the sauce

    Pour in the cooking cream and add black pepper. Simmer gently, then stir in grated parmesan.

  4. Combine everything

    Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and toss well. Add a little reserved pasta water if the sauce is too thick.

  5. Garnish and serve

    Finish with fresh parsley, a little parmesan and freshly ground black pepper. Serve warm.

✦

Chef's Tip: Save a little pasta water before draining. The starchy water helps create a smooth, glossy and creamy sauce.
